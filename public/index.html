ABOUT SPAZA

South Africa's community-first online marketplace — built by locals, for locals.

OUR STORY

Spaza was born from a simple idea: every South African deserves access to a world-class online shopping experience, and every South African entrepreneur deserves a fair platform to grow their business.

Named after the beloved neighbourhood spaza shops that have long been the heartbeat of our communities, Spaza the platform brings that same spirit of entrepreneurship, accessibility, and trust into the digital age.

We are proudly South African, committed to growing local businesses, creating jobs, and delivering convenience to every corner of our country.

OUR MISSION

To democratise e-commerce in South Africa — making it effortless for anyone to buy and sell online, safely and affordably.

We partner with trusted South African payment infrastructure including PayFast to ensure every transaction is secure, compliant, and reliable for both buyers and sellers.

"Empowering South African commerce, one transaction at a time."

1. Your Right to Return (Section 56, Consumer Protection Act)

In accordance with the Consumer Protection Act 68 of 2008, you have the right to return goods and receive a full refund if the product is defective, does not match its description, or is not fit for its intended purpose. This right applies for a period of 6 months from the date of delivery.

2. Our 30-Day Voluntary Returns Policy

In addition to your statutory rights, Spaza offers a voluntary 30-day returns window from the date of delivery. To qualify:

  • The item must be unused and in its original condition
  • All original packaging, tags, and accessories must be included
  • Proof of purchase (order number) must be provided
  • The return must be initiated within 30 days of the delivery date

3. Items Excluded from Returns

  • Perishable goods (food, fresh produce)
  • Digital products and software licences once downloaded
  • Hygiene products (underwear, swimwear) unless sealed and unused
  • Customised or personalised items
  • Hazardous materials

4. How to Initiate a Return

  • Email navhani.sky@gmail.com with your order number, reason for return, and photos of the item
  • Our team will respond within 2 business days with a returns authorisation number
  • Package the item securely and ship to: 5488 Oregon Crescent, Crystal Park, Benoni, 1501
  • Once received and inspected, refunds are processed within 7 business days

5. Refund Method

Refunds are issued via the original payment method. For PayFast transactions, refunds are processed back to the original card or bank account. Please allow 3–5 business days after processing for funds to reflect.

6. Damaged or Incorrect Items

If you receive a damaged or incorrect item, notify us at navhani.sky@gmail.com within 48 hours of delivery with photographs. We will arrange a collection and replacement or full refund at no cost to you.

7. Return Shipping Costs

For defective, damaged, or incorrectly dispatched items, Spaza covers return shipping costs. For change-of-mind returns (within the 30-day window), the buyer is responsible for return shipping unless otherwise agreed.

8. Seller-Specific Policies

Third-party sellers on the Spaza platform may have their own returns policies. These are displayed on each product listing. However, all sellers must comply with the minimum standards set out in this policy and the Consumer Protection Act.
